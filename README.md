# ERS Brazil — Design System

> **Environmental Recovery Solutions Brazil**
> *"DO WHAT'S RIGHT"* — Transformando eletrônicos em soluções sustentáveis para um futuro responsável.

This repository is a living design system for **ERS Brazil**. It contains the brand's
visual foundations (color, type, spacing, elevation), real brand assets (logos, badges,
imagery), reusable UI-kit components, and sample slide layouts — everything an agent or
designer needs to produce on-brand interfaces, decks, and marketing material.

---

## 1. Company & product context

**ERS Brazil** (Environmental Recovery Solutions Brazil) is the Brazilian operation of a
**Canadian company with 25+ years of experience**, active in Brazil **since 2014**. ERS
specializes in the **recycling and life-cycle extension of electronics** — e-waste,
IT asset disposition, and reverse logistics — operating within a **circular-economy**
model where technology, innovation and sustainability move together.

- **Headquarters / plant:** Indaiatuba — SP, Brazil. ~**5,000 m²** facility, capacity up to **400 tonnes/month**.
- **Positioning (current):** *"Somos uma empresa especializada no aumento do ciclo de vida e reciclagem de eletrônicos."* (A company specialized in extending the life cycle and recycling of electronics — note the strategic shift **away from "waste management"** toward **value creation**.)
- **Purpose:** Transform e-waste management into a circular economy through innovation, environmental responsibility and operational excellence — building a sustainable, inclusive future.
- **Vision:** Be a **global reference** in circular economy, sustainable innovation and operational excellence in the e-waste sector **by 2030**.
- **Values:** Compromisso Ambiental · Inovação · Governança Transparente · Responsabilidade Social · Integridade · Colaboração · Foco no Cliente · Transparência.

### Services (the three pillars)
1. **ITAD / IT Asset Disposition** — end-to-end disposition of IT assets. *Purchase of obsolete assets · device remanufacturing · guaranteed data sanitization.*
2. **Reciclagem (Recycling)** — for companies disposing of technological/obsolete waste. *Sustainability report · secure collection across Brazil · guaranteed data sanitization.*
3. **Logística Reversa (Reverse Logistics)** — environmentally responsible return of waste back into the production chain. *Secure collection across Brazil · enterprise-focused.*

### Differentiators
- **Data sanitization** to global standards: **Blancco** (logical wipe, world leader), physical fragmentation down to **6 mm³** (irrecoverable), **DoD 5220.22-M** & **NIST 800-88**, full **LGPD** compliance, individual technical reports & certificates.
- **TAPA** (Transported Asset Protection Association) security level · **24h monitoring** incl. thermal cameras · **point-to-point cargo tracking** · **R$ 1.5M+** invested in security infrastructure.
- **End-to-end traceability** · **Zero resíduo / zero aterro / zero incineração** (zero waste / landfill / incineration).
- **Certifications:** **R2v3** (Responsible Recycling), **ISO 9001** (Quality), **ISO 14001** (Environmental), **ISO 45001** (Health & Safety), **ISO 27001** (Data Security).

### Proof / cases
- **ITAD for TJSP** (São Paulo State Court): **+150k** items collected (computers, monitors, desktops, servers), **R$ 45M** in goods collected & negotiated, **18-month** project.
- **Reverse logistics:** **27 states + Federal District**, **5,200 branches** served, **5-year** project.

### Brand evolution note
The 2025 rebrand deliberately **moved away from the classic recycling symbol / organic-waste
association**, repositioning ERS in the **e-waste** space. The name shifted from *"ERS do
Brasil — Eletronics Recycling Services"* to **"ERS Brazil — Environmental Recovery
Solutions Brazil"**, broadening scope while preserving brand recognition. The redesign was
produced by **MF Marketing** (Direção de Arte: Andresa Fernandes; Designers: Bianca & Bruna Peixoto).

## 2. Content fundamentals (voice & copy)

**Languages.** Primary language is **Brazilian Portuguese**. English appears in two
intentional places: the **tagline "DO WHAT'S RIGHT"** and parts of the **name/descriptor**
("Environmental Recovery Solutions"). Technical/industry terms stay in their standard form
(ITAD, e-waste, LGPD, ESG, Blancco, R2v3).

**Tone.** Confident, technical, and reassuring. ERS sells **trust, security, and
compliance** to enterprise/government buyers — so copy leans on **proof, certifications and
numbers**, not hype. It is corporate but purpose-driven (sustainability is the moral
backbone, never greenwashed fluff).

**Person & address.** Speaks as **"nós/nossa" (we)** about the company, and addresses the
reader as **"sua empresa / você" (your company / you)**. Marketing copy is direct and
provocative in the hook, then resolves into calm authority.

**Casing.**
- **Eyebrows / section labels / pills:** ALL CAPS, Montserrat Semi Bold, letter-spaced (e.g. `O QUE ENTREGAMOS`, `NOSSOS SERVIÇOS`, `CASES DE SUCESSO`).
- **Tagline:** ALL CAPS, Bebas Neue, only `DO WHAT'S RIGHT`.
- **Headlines:** sentence case, often with a **green** or **orange** keyword highlight.
- **Body:** sentence case.

**Highlighting.** A single phrase in a sentence is colored **green** (positive/brand) or
**orange** (risk/attention) to carry the emphasis — e.g. *"...continuam armazenando dados
sensíveis. **E é exatamente aí que mora o risco.**"* Bold is used sparingly for the punch line.

**Emoji.** **None.** The brand never uses emoji. Do not introduce them.

**Vibe.** "Do what's right." Responsible, grown-up, slightly bold. Think *certified
industrial sustainability* — clean glass-and-green facade, not crunchy-eco. Risk-aware
(it leads with the danger of un-sanitized data) then offers ERS as the certified answer.

### Representative copy (lift tone, not verbatim)
- Hook: *"Seus eletrônicos parados ainda carregam riscos que sua empresa não pode ignorar."*
- Value: *"Enquanto o mercado foca apenas no valor de revenda, a ERS atua com controle total do processo."*
- Pillars framed as a two-column promise: **"O QUE ENTREGAMOS"** ↔ **"O QUE SUA EMPRESA GANHA"**.
- CTAs (green buttons): *"Quero proteger meus dados", "Solicitar uma proposta", "Solicitar avaliação", "Descobrir o valor do meu ativo agora!"*
- Slogan: *"Transformando eletrônicos em soluções sustentáveis para um futuro responsável."*

## 3. Visual foundations

### Color
- **Petrol `#043035`** is THE brand color — used **intensively** as the dominant dark surface (heroes, dark sections, footers, the truck). Treat it as "the company color."
- **Green `#65CE21`** = life, recycling, circular economy. Used for the logo, **CTA buttons**, keyword highlights, and large Bebas display type on petrol.
- **Orange `#F4762F`** = energy / attention. Used for **eyebrow pills**, the full-width **certification band**, risk callouts, and the occasional highlighted word. It is a true accent — small doses.
- **Grays `#BFBFBF` / `#999999`** = supporting neutrals (descriptor text, captions, dividers, muted backgrounds).
- Pairings: green on petrol, white on petrol, orange band as a divider, white cards on petrol or on off-white. Avoid green text on white at small sizes (low contrast) — use petrol.
- ⚠️ The `Paleta de cores` PNG shows slightly punchier swatches (`#042c34 / #04cc04 / #c4c4c4 / #9c9c9c / #fc7424`); the **brand manual values above are authoritative** and used throughout this system.

### Type
- **Montserrat** is the official typeface for **all running text** (Light → Black). **Semi Bold** is the brand's signature weight for labels/headings. Set caps labels with `0.12em` tracking.
- **Bebas Neue** is reserved for the **tagline and big display moments** only (uppercase, condensed, high-impact).
- The **logo "ERS"** uses a **proprietary custom face** (circular strokes, Gestalt arrows in the "S"). It is **not a system font** — always use the supplied **logo assets**, never re-typeset "ERS." The canonical bank lives in `assets/logos/` as **vector SVGs** (plus high-res transparent PNGs), tightly cropped with **no surrounding border** — prefer these so the mark sits flush and reads at a generous size.
- **Logo preference (client direction):** lead with the **ERS BRAZIL mark only** — the lockup *without* the "Environmental Recovery Solutions" descriptor line — and use it at a **generous size**. Files: `logo-primary-color.png` / `-white` / `-black`. The full descriptor lockup (`logo-full-*`) is secondary, for formal/legal contexts only.

### Backgrounds & imagery
- **Two background modes:** deep **petrol** sections and **off-white/white** sections, alternating for rhythm. A single **orange band** breaks long stretches.
- **Real operation photography is the backbone of the visual narrative** (`assets/photos/`). Prefer authentic facility/process shots over stock or conceptual renders. The library covers the full flow: receiving → cataloging → sorting → **Blancco data wipe** (the wall of monitors with red progress rings) → **TAPA security** (CCTV wall) → fragmentation/shredder → shredded residue → warehouse/stock. Use them to tell one continuous story: *secure, traceable, certified recovery*.
- On petrol heroes, place a real photo in a rounded panel with a dark gradient scrim so green/white text reads; a darkened wide shot (e.g. `warehouse.jpg`) works as a full-bleed background at ~30% opacity.
- **Don't use stock/bank imagery for the "o que o mercado tradicional ignora" differentiators** — that block is **iconography only** (Lucide on green tint discs). Photos belong in the hero and the "Dentro da operação" process strip.
- Photography vibe: **cool, clean, slightly tech/industrial**; real equipment, hi-vis vests, green safety mesh, motherboards, shredded e-waste. Not warm, not grainy.
- **Pattern (S-Partner):** the stylized **"S" arrows** are the brand's supporting graphic, supplied as official assets in `assets/pattern/` — solid marks `s-petrol.png` / `s-green.png` / `s-white.png` and a ready-made **degrade texture** (`degrade-texture.png`, a fading S-field). Apply them as **negative backgrounds at 10–30% opacity**: pick the color that contrasts its surface (s-white on petrol/green, s-petrol or s-green on light), often **cropped** off an edge. Use the **degrade texture** for larger composition areas (petrol panels, hero bands, certificate sidebars, slide backgrounds). Keep it subtle — a watermark/texture, never busy. Do not re-typeset the “S” as a glyph; use the PNGs.

### Flags (Brazil · Canada)
ERS is a **Canadian group with a Brazilian operation** — that provenance is shown with a flag
pair, mandatory in the footer of every document (`documents/*.html`, `.ers-flags` in
`documents/doc.css`). Real flag PNGs live in `assets/flags/` (`br.png`, `ca.png`, 160 px wide).
**Order is fixed: Brazil first, then Canada.** Render at **21×14 px** with a 6 px gap and
**no country names** beside or near them; hairline ring in white on petrol, petrol on light.
It is a small **provenance mark** — never scaled up to compete with the logo, and never used
in place of the logo.

**Canonical postal address:** Alameda Plutão, 555 · American Park Empresarial NR ·
Indaiatuba — SP · CEP 13347-656.

### The symbol & its meaning
The "S" is built from **arrows that go and return** — the circular economy: continuous flow,
transformation, return. "S" also stands for **Solutions**. Lean into motion/cycle metaphors,
never the generic ♻ recycling glyph.

### Layout
- Generous **vertical rhythm**; full-bleed colored sections stacked vertically.
- Content max-width ~1100–1200px, centered.
- Two-column promise/comparison blocks; 2- and 3-up card grids.
- Eyebrow pill → headline → supporting paragraph → CTA is the recurring stack.
- Large dark **form/CTA panel** with a faint oversized `DO WHAT'S RIGHT` watermark behind it.

### Corner radii, cards, borders
- **Cards:** white fill, **6px** radius, **soft shadow** (`--shadow-md`), thin or no border. Image tiles get a slightly larger radius + heavier shadow (`--shadow-card`).
- **Pills/buttons:** fully rounded (`999px`) for the orange eyebrow labels; green CTA buttons use ~`6px` (manual landing comp) — both are in the kit.
- **Borders:** hairline `#e4e7e6` on light; `rgba(255,255,255,.14)` on petrol.

### Shadows / elevation
Soft, petrol-tinted shadows (not neutral black). Three steps (`sm/md/lg`) plus a card and an
on-dark variant in `colors_and_type.css`. No hard/長 drop shadows, no neumorphism.

### Motion & states
- **Easing:** gentle ease-out (`--ease-out`). **Durations** 140–400ms.
- **Hover:** buttons darken slightly (green→`--green-600`, orange→`--orange-600`) and lift 1–2px; cards raise shadow `md → lg`; image tiles scale image ~1.03 under a clipped corner.
- **Press:** subtle scale-down (`0.98`) — no color flash.
- **Entrances:** simple fades / short rises. No bounce, no parallax circus. Keep it corporate-calm.

### Transparency & blur
Used sparingly: dark **gradient scrims** over hero/tile imagery for legibility; occasional
low-opacity petrol overlay. No heavy glassmorphism.

## 4. Iconography

ERS has **no proprietary icon font or custom icon set** in the supplied materials. Observed usage:

- **Check/confirmation marks:** small **orange filled circle with a white check** for benefit/risk lists (landing comp). Recreate with a CDN icon inside an orange disc.
- **Certification marks** are **real third-party badges** (ISO 9001/14001/45001, R2v3) shown as outlined circular seals — these are **brand/compliance assets**, not UI icons. Represent ISO/R2 as labeled circular tokens; do not fake official seals as if certified beyond what's documented.
- **Pin/location, GPS, shield, document** concepts appear as supporting graphics — line icons, medium stroke.
- **The "S-arrows"** double as an iconographic motif (circular economy), but it is the **logo mark**, used from PNG — not redrawn.
- **No emoji. No unicode-glyph icons.**

**Recommended icon set for this system:** **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)** (CDN), 2px stroke,
rounded — clean, modern, neutral, matching the Montserrat/tech-industrial tone. Load via
`https://unpkg.com/lucide@latest`. This is a **substitution** (the brand ships no icon set);
choose Lucide glyphs that read as: `shield-check`, `recycle`/`refresh-cw`, `map-pin`,
`file-check`, `truck`, `hard-drive`, `cpu`, `leaf`, `badge-check`, `lock`. **Flag to the
client** if exact icon fidelity matters.

## 6. Substitutions & caveats (please review)
- **Logo wordmark font is proprietary** and not supplied as a font file → all "ERS" lockups use the **PNG logos**. ✅ no quality loss for placement, but you cannot typeset new text in that face.
- **Montserrat & Bebas Neue** load from **Google Fonts CDN** (both are the actual brand fonts, freely available) — no substitution, but no local `.ttf` is bundled. Ask if you need offline/embedded font files.
- **Icons:** no brand set exists → **Lucide** substituted (see §4). Flag if exact fidelity is needed.
- **No codebase / Figma / live URL** was provided; UI kit is reconstructed from the landing comp + deck + manual. Pixel spacing is interpreted, not measured.
- Client logos in the landing "trusted by" row (Dell, Google, Microsoft, Netflix, Banco do Brasil) are **third-party marks** shown for reference; the kit renders neutral text placeholders rather than embedding other companies' logos.
